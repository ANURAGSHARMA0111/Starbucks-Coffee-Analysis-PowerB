# Starbucks-Coffee-Analysis-PowerBI

## 📌 Business Questions

1. What is the total number of beverages in the dataset?
2. What is the average sugar content of Starbucks beverages?
3. What is the average calorie content of Starbucks beverages?
4. What is the average caffeine content of Starbucks beverages?
5. What is the average calorie content across different beverage categories?
6. How are beverages distributed across different categories?
7. Which beverage categories have the highest average caffeine content?
8. Which are the Top 5 beverages with the highest caffeine content?
9. How do beverage preparation methods affect the analysis?
10. How does the protein range relate to beverage characteristics?

Starbucks Coffee Analysis is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Starbucks coffee beverages and uncover insights related to calories, sugar, caffeine, beverage categories, and preparation methods. The dashboard uses KPIs and interactive visualizations to make the data easy to understand and support data-driven analysis.
